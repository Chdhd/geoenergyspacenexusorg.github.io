how the institute is organized

Three pillars, one institute

Every project, publication, and partnership at GESNRI sits under one of three pillars — used throughout the site to help you find work in your area.

Pillar I

Space & spatial data

Earth observation, satellite imagery, and geospatial analysis for land, water, and infrastructure decisions.

Pillar II

Humanitarian, climate & energy

Climate intelligence and sustainable-energy research applied to humanitarian and development needs.

Pillar III

Space exploration

Exploration science, mission concepts, and payload research pursued with regional and international partners.
